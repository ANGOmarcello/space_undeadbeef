# Credits and notices

The original Space Deadbeef game, executable, artwork, audio and associated
material belong to their respective creators and rights holders. The restored
game retains the original creator credits. The native restoration and
modernization are by Angelo Cammalleri; see [Credits.md](Documentation/Credits.md).
The original content is not relicensed by this source package.

The restoration workflow follows [Respring](https://respring.dev/) and Dylan
McDonald's [legacy restoration skill](https://github.com/DylanMcD8/respring-skill).
The release layout and Files-based progress approach follow the public Chuzzle
and Peggle examples. This project implements its own game interpreter and
PSH0 progress adapter.

The offline sprite preparation utilities and their license are in
[`script/tools`](script/tools/). They use xBRZ offline as described in
[RetinaSprites.md](Documentation/RetinaSprites.md) and [RetinaShip.md](Documentation/RetinaShip.md).
The scaler is not bundled in the app. Original and derived artwork provenance
is recorded in those documents and [AppIcon.md](Documentation/AppIcon.md).
